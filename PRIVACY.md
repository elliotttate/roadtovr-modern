# Privacy policy

**Effective date:** August 27, 2026

Road to VR — Horizon is an unofficial Chrome extension that restyles pages on `roadtovr.com` into a modern reading experience. This policy explains the limited information the extension handles and how it is used.

## Information handled by the extension

The extension processes the content and URL of the Road to VR page currently open in the browser so it can rebuild that page's presentation. It may also store:

- Reader preferences such as whether the redesign is enabled, theme, text size, and reading width.
- Stories the user intentionally saves, including the Road to VR URL, headline, image URL, and the time the story was saved.
- A small local cache of featured-image URLs obtained from Road to VR's own WordPress API, used to restore artwork on story cards.

The extension does not collect names, email addresses, authentication information, payment information, personal communications, precise location, health information, or browsing history outside Road to VR.

## How information is used

This information is used only to provide the extension's visible features: restyling Road to VR, applying reader preferences, showing the optional saved-story list, and restoring missing story artwork.

Preferences are stored with Chrome's synchronized storage and may be synchronized by Chrome through the user's Google account, subject to Google's own privacy terms. Saved stories and the featured-image cache remain in Chrome's local extension storage on the user's device. Users can clear saved stories from the extension popup and can remove all extension data by uninstalling the extension.

## Collection, sharing, and sale

The developer does not operate a server for this extension and does not receive extension data. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry, tracking pixels, or remote code. The developer does not sell, rent, share, or transfer user data.

Road to VR pages, images, links, and the publisher's existing Disqus discussion may make their normal network requests to Road to VR, Disqus, or a destination the user chooses to open. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies; the extension does not add user data to those requests.

## Security and limited use

The extension is limited to Road to VR and requests only Chrome's `storage` permission plus access to Road to VR's `http` and `https` origins. Any use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data/), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository with an updated effective date.

## Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the project's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/elliotttate/roadtovr-modern/issues) page. Security vulnerabilities should be reported using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
